Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 2026

Introduction

NTH Bizz is a Property & Rent Management application developed to help property owners manage properties, tenants, rent collection and reports.

Information We Collect

The app stores only the information entered by you, including:

  • Property Details
  • Tenant Details
  • Rent Collection Records
  • Payment History
  • Generated PDF Receipts

Local Storage

All information is stored locally on your device. NTH Bizz does not upload your data to our servers.

Internet Usage

The application works completely offline. Internet connection is not required for normal usage.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer your personal information to any third party.

Security

Your information remains stored on your own device. You are responsible for keeping backups of important data.

Children's Privacy

NTH Bizz is intended for business use and is not designed for children.

Changes to this Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any future changes will be reflected on this page.
